About me

Software Developer, Entrepreneur & Computer Science Educator

I am a Software Developer, Entrepreneur, and Computer Science Educator, currently working full-time as a Computer Science Educator at R.E.S. Polytechnic. As the Founder & CEO of Inoglle and creator of ADV Indian Coder, I focus on building innovative software solutions, empowering developers, and making technology education accessible through practical, project-based learning.

Before joining RES Polytechnic, I visited around 20 engineering colleges in Chennai, Bangalore, Hyderabad and Mumbai as a guest lecturer and career guidance speaker. In every college I saw the same problem. Students did not know what they should have learned before joining engineering, and many were confused about their careers. Even third-year and fourth-year students did not have a LinkedIn or GitHub account. They did not know how to build a professional network, and they did not know what the IT industry actually expects. The college syllabus and the skills needed for an IT job are very different, and most students find this out too late. Because of this, many engineers struggle to find jobs after graduation.

I realised that guiding students after they join engineering is already late. So I decided to work with polytechnic students. Many of them go for higher studies like BTech after their diploma. If I teach 100 students and 50 of them go to BTech, those 50 will already know what to learn, which skills to build and how to prepare for the industry before they even enter engineering. That can change their future.

I already had a job, but I chose to join RES Polytechnic. For me, money is not the first priority. My goal is to help students succeed in life. Alongside teaching, I am also building my startup and my online content, and I am growing my network. I believe that when I have a strong network and experience, success and money will follow. But my main aim is always to guide students.
